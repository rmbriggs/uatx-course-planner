### PARAGRAPHS ###
[normal] Overview
[normal] The UATX 2026-2027 Academic Catalog includes several changes that afford students additional program clarity, flexibility, and opportunity. Students in Classes 2028 and 2029 may elect to this new 2026-2027 Bachelor’s of Arts in Liberal Studies program to gain the benefits of the new program design, or they may continue with the program in their original catalog.
[normal] The new program has three main pillars (same as previous):
[normal] Intellectual Foundations,
[normal] Liberal Studies Major (which includes Centers/concentrations and electives), and
[normal] Polaris
[normal] The following table below outlines some of the differences:
[normal] Regarding Centers and concentrations:
[normal] The old program requires students to elect a Center (CAL, CEPH, or CSTEM) and to complete a Center core. Students may (but are not required to) choose a concentration within that Center (4 total concentrations).
[normal] The new program requires neither Center election nor completion of a Center core.  Students may (but are not required to) choose from 7 different different academic concentrations and 1 applied track. The number of credits required to complete a concentration (36) makes the possibility of completing more than one concentration feasible.
[normal] More details about these differences, as well as mappings that show how old-program courses
[normal] will count for credit in the new program, are in the full-length document..
